    01 — Tree surgery

    We never touch a tree before we’ve surveyed it.

    All our tradesmen are fully trained with many years’ experience. Health and Safety are highly important to us, and we will never begin work on a tree without first carrying out a site survey to ensure safety to both our staff and the public.

    02 — Felling & removal

    Down in sections. Over the roof, not through it.

    Straight fell where there’s room to drop it, sectional dismantle and rigging where there isn’t — lowering each piece on ropes over sheds, conservatories, greenhouses and next door’s fence.

    • Tree removal & felling
    • Sectional dismantling
    • Rigging
    • Emergency call outs

    03 — Stump grinding

    The stump comes out too.

    Ground out below ground level, not cut flush and left for you to trip over. Brash chipped on site, timber cut and stacked or taken away, and the ground raked off behind us.

    • Stump Grinding
    • Site Clearance
    • Brash chipped on site
    • Timber removed
