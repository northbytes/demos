Comprehensive arboricultural and land-based services.

Redwood Tree & Land Services provide comprehensive arboricultural and land-based services in Essex and the surrounding counties.

Before anything else

All our tradesmen are fully trained with many years’ experience. Health and Safety are highly important to us, and we will never begin work on a tree without first carrying out a site survey to ensure safety to both our staff and the public.

Tree services

From minor pruning to complete removal.

01

Tree Surgery

Crown reduction, thinning, crown lifting, deadwooding and formative pruning — the work that keeps a tree safe and in proportion to the space it sits in, rather than taking it out.

02

Tree Removal & Felling

Straight fell where there’s room, sectional dismantle and rigging where there isn’t — over sheds, conservatories, greenhouses and neighbouring gardens.

03

Stump Grinding

The stump ground out below ground level so the space can be turfed, planted or paved. Not left as a trip hazard for somebody else to deal with.

04

Site Clearance

Brash chipped on site, timber cut down and stacked or taken away, and the ground left clear — whether that’s a domestic garden or a whole plot.

05

Vegetation Management

Overgrown boundaries, scrub, ivy, hedgerow and self-seeded growth brought back under control, as a one-off clearance or on an ongoing basis.

06

Tree Planting

Species selection, planting and establishment — including replacement planting where a tree has had to come out.

07

Tree Surveys

A site survey before any work begins, every time. Condition, hazard and the case for what should actually be done — which is sometimes less than you expected.

08

TPO Applications

If a tree is protected or you’re in a conservation area, the application goes to the council before a saw comes out. We handle it.

09

Emergency Call Outs

Storm damage, failed limbs and trees down across drives, roofs and boundaries — made safe first, tidied after.

Additional services

The ground doesn’t have to wait for a second contractor.

We also carry out soft landscaping works, including turf laying, garden clearance and maintenance.

10

Turfing

Ground prepared, levelled and turfed — commonly straight after a removal, so the garden goes back to lawn rather than sitting as churned soil.

11

Garden Clearance

Full clearance of neglected and overgrown gardens, from waist-high growth back to workable ground.

12

Land-Based Services

Wider land and grounds work beyond the domestic garden, across Essex and the surrounding counties.
