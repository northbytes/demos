Professional arboriculture contractors, based in Rayleigh.

Based in Rayleigh, we are a professional arboriculture contractors providing complete tree and land-based services to customers in Essex, Brentwood and the surrounding counties.

Our specialities

Trained, ticketed and audited.

All our arborists are fully trained with many years’ experience. As fully trained arborists, we can carry out any tree work you may require, from minor pruning to complete tree removal and felling.

Health and Safety are highly important to us, and we will never begin work on a tree without first carrying out a site survey to ensure safety to both our staff and the public.

Redwood Tree & Land Services hold full public liability insurance and all work is guaranteed.

How we work

A thorough discussion before a single cut.

For any work we are involved in, we always take care to have a thorough discussion with our customers, to ensure we understand their exact requirements. This allows us to carry out the work they specify efficiently and effectively.

01

You call

Tell us roughly what you’re looking at — how many trees, how big, how close to the house. Quotations are free.

02

We survey

We come and look before we quote. Sometimes the answer is a reduction rather than a removal, and that’s a cheaper job for you.

03

We agree the work

Including whether the tree is protected. If a TPO or conservation area applies, the application goes in first.

04

We do it

Trained, ticketed arborists, correct rigging, full insurance, and the public and your property kept safe throughout.

05

We clear up

Brash chipped, timber stacked or taken, stump ground out. The site goes back to you clear.

06

And if you want — we finish it

Turf laid, ground cleared, planting done. The bit most tree firms hand to somebody else.
