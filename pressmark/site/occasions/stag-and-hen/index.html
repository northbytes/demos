WHAT PEOPLE ASK

Before you order.

Can everyone have a different name on the back?

Yes. Send the names and sizes in a list and we'll match them up. Names are £4.00 a shirt on top, whatever the run size, and we'll show you every one on the proof before we print.

What if someone drops out?

Tell us before you approve the proof and we'll take them off. After that it's printed, and you've got a spare — which is why we suggest ordering one anyway.

How late can I order?

Five working days from proof approval, so ten days out is comfortable. Express is 48 hours for 15% more. Tighter than that, ring us and we'll tell you honestly whether it'll make it.

Can you print a photo of his face?

Yes — that's DTG, full colour, no extra cost per colour. Send the biggest version of the photo you have. One taken close up on a phone is fine; one screenshotted off Instagram usually isn't, and we'll say so before you pay.
