## 2. Brand identity

### Colour palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Primary | **Hartwood Green** | `#1E3A2F` | Headers, nav, footer, primary buttons |
| Accent | **Oak Amber** | `#C9862B` | CTAs, links, highlights, icons |
| Background | **Limewash** | `#F6F2E9` | Page background, cards |
| Surface | **Plaster White** | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on limewash, form fields |
| Text | **Charcoal** | `#26251F` | Body text |
| Muted | **Sage Grey** | `#7C8A7E` | Captions, dividers, secondary text |

Dark sections: Hartwood Green background, Limewash text, Oak Amber accents.
Never use pure black; Charcoal only. Amber is an accent — max ~10% of any screen.

### Typography

- **Headings:** Fraunces (Google Fonts) — 600/700, tight tracking. Warm, confident, craft feel.
- **Body / UI:** Inter (Google Fonts) — 400/500/600.
- Numerals in stats/pricing: Fraunces for display sizes, Inter elsewhere.

### Logo direction

Wordmark "HARTWOOD" in Fraunces with a simple house-gable / roofline stroke over the "A", "BUILDERS · EST. 1998" in small Inter caps beneath. Monochrome versions: all-green on light, all-limewash on dark. No gradients, no 3D.

### Voice & tone

Plain-spoken, warm, unflashy. Tradesperson confidence, not marketing gloss.
- Say: "We'll give you a fixed price in writing before we lift a tool."
- Don't say: "Leveraging end-to-end construction solutions."
Short sentences. First person plural ("we"). British English.

### Imagery direction

Real-feeling site photography: crews at work, scaffolding at golden hour, oak beams, sawdust, finished kitchen/extension interiors with natural light. Warm tones matching the palette. No stocky hard-hat handshakes, no glass skyscrapers.
