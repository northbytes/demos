Questions we get asked

Including the awkward one about how new the company is.

Still not sure?

Ring us and ask. We're on site most days — leave a message and we'll call back the same evening.

020 7946 0142
You were incorporated in 2025 — how do I know you're any good?

Fair question, and we'd rather you asked it. Delux Construction is new as a company; the people are not. We set it up specifically to put building, electrical and plumbing work under one roof instead of subcontracting it out. Ask us for addresses of finished jobs and we'll give you them.

Do you really do the electrics and plumbing yourselves?

Yes. We're registered for electrical installation and for plumbing, heating and air-conditioning as well as domestic building work. All certification is supplied on completion.

Do you handle planning and building control?

Yes — drawings, planning or permitted development, structural calculations and building control sign-off. Most side-returns go through under permitted development.

How do you price?

Fixed written quote after a site visit and drawings, itemised by stage. Staged payments against work completed. No deposit before we start on site.

Are you insured?

£5m public liability, employer's liability, and all work guaranteed. Certificates on request.

Which areas do you cover?

Penge, Anerley, Beckenham, Sydenham, Crystal Palace, Forest Hill, Dulwich, Bromley and Catford — broadly SE19, SE20, SE23, SE26 and BR1–BR3. Ask if you're just outside.
