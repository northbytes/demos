The company is new. The trades aren't.

Delux Construction Ltd was registered in March 2025. Anyone can check that on Companies House, so we'd rather say it ourselves than have you find it.

We set the company up for one reason: to put building work, electrical installation and plumbing and heating under a single roof, instead of subcontracting two of the three out and hoping everyone turns up in the right order.

If you've had building work done before, you'll know the pattern. The builder finishes first fix and goes quiet. The electrician is on another job for a fortnight. The plumber turns up before the electrician and can't get at the wall. Nobody is quite responsible, and your kitchen is a building site for three weeks longer than anyone quoted.

That's not a people problem, it's a structure problem — and it's the reason we registered all three trades from day one. One team, one point of contact and one programme, from the day the footings go in to the day the lights go on. If something doesn't line up, it's ours to fix. There's nobody to point at.

We work on the housing stock this part of London is actually built from: Victorian and Edwardian terraces and semis across Penge, Anerley, Beckenham, Sydenham, Crystal Palace, Forest Hill, Dulwich and Bromley. Side returns, rear extensions, dormer lofts, whole-house refurbishments. We know what's behind the plaster in these houses because we're in one most weeks.

How we work

Four steps, no surprises in the middle of them.

  1. Free site visit

    We come and look at the actual job. No charge, no obligation, and we'll tell you straight if what you want doesn't fit what you want to spend.

  2. Fixed written price

    Itemised by stage so you can see what each part costs. Drawings, structural calculations and building control handled if you need them.

  3. One team on site

    Builders, electricians and plumbers all ours, working to one programme. Site swept every evening, one number to ring when you have a question.

  4. Sign-off and certificates

    Building control sign-off, electrical certification and heating commissioning documents handed over with the keys. Staged payments against work completed.
