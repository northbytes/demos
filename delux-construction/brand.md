**The read:** three SIC codes on a domestic builder is the whole proposition.
Most small builders sub out electrics and plumbing, which is where homeowners
get burned — the "he's waiting on the sparky" delay is the single most common
extension horror story. Delux registered all three trades on day one. The site
leads on that.

**The proportional job.** A March-2025 firm in SE20 is not building mansions.
Its bread and butter is the SE London Victorian/Edwardian terrace: side-return
and rear kitchen extensions, loft conversions, full refurbs, bathrooms and
kitchens, rewires and boiler swaps. Every visual on the site is that house —
London stock brick, bifolds, roof lantern, small walled garden.

**Service area:** Penge, Anerley, Beckenham, Sydenham, Crystal Palace, Forest
Hill, Dulwich, Bromley, Catford — roughly SE19/SE20/SE23/SE26/BR1–BR3.

## 2. Positioning

**Tagline:** **"Builder, electrician and plumber. One firm."**

Alt: "The whole job, under one roof." · "No waiting on the sparky."

**One-liner (hero):**
Delux Construction builds extensions, loft conversions and full refurbishments
across South East London — with our own electricians and plumbers on the team,
so your job never stops waiting for a trade to turn up.

**About / story:**
Delux Construction is a South East London building firm working across Penge,
Beckenham, Sydenham and Bromley. We do extensions, loft conversions and whole-house
refurbishments on the kind of Victorian terraces this part of London is built from —
and unlike most builders our size, we carry our own electricians and plumbers.
That means one team, one point of contact and one programme from the day the
footings are dug to the day the lights go on. No three-week gap waiting for a
sparky to have a window. No finger-pointing when something doesn't line up.

**Handling the "new company" objection — head on, don't hide it.**
Companies House says March 2025 and anyone can check. So the site says it first:
*"The company is new. The trades aren't."* Founded by people with years on the
tools, incorporated as a single firm to bring all three trades under one roof.
Owning it reads as confidence; hiding it and getting caught reads as the opposite.

## 3. Brand identity

Pulled from the hero video's own palette — blue-hour London sky, warm light
spilling out of a finished extension. The site and the film are one thing.

### Colour

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Primary | **Blue Hour** | `#141C2B` | Nav, footer, dark sections, headings |
| Primary deep | **Night** | `#0B1119` | Video scrims, deepest surfaces |
| Accent | **Worklight** | `#F5A524` | CTAs, links, the "on" state, stage markers |
| Paper | **Plaster** | `#F4F1EC` | Page background |
| Surface | **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on plaster, form fields |
| Text | **Ink** | `#171A1F` | Body copy |
| Muted | **Slate** | `#6B7280` | Captions, meta, dividers |
| Brick | **Stock** | `#B08968` | Rare warm tint — section rules, illustration only |

Never pure black. Worklight amber is a signal colour, max ~10% of any screen —
it marks the thing you're meant to click and the trade currently "live" in the
build sequence. Dark sections: Blue Hour ground, Plaster text, Worklight accents.

### Typography

- **Display:** Archivo (Google Fonts) — 600/700, tight tracking, slightly
  expanded. Industrial, signage-like, confident. Deliberately *not* a warm
  craft serif; this is a city firm, not a country joinery.
- **Body / UI:** Inter — 400/500/600.
- Numerals: Archivo tabular for stats and the stage timeline.

### Logo direction

Wordmark **DELUX** in Archivo 700, wide tracking, with **CONSTRUCTION** in small
Inter caps beneath at matched width. Optional mark: three stacked horizontal bars
of decreasing width (build / electrical / plumbing) — reads as a roofline and as
the three trades. Monochrome only: ink on plaster, plaster on blue hour.

### Voice

Direct, London, unpretentious, specific. Numbers over adjectives.
- Say: "Fixed written price before we start. Your own project manager. Site swept every evening."
- Say: "We're a new company. Between us we've been doing this a long time."
- Don't say: "Bespoke luxury living solutions." "Passionate about quality."

## 4. What the site must do

Straight from the client's own words — *"people can visit to see my previous job.
To book a job, contacting or requesting for a quote"*:

1. **See previous work** — the portfolio is the primary object. They have no photo
   library yet, so the hero build sequence carries the proof until real job photos
   replace it. Every project card is a slot waiting for a real photo.
2. **Request a quote** — the main conversion. Multi-step, low friction, asks the
   things a builder actually needs to price a job.
3. **Book a job / site visit** — secondary CTA, a date preference on the quote form
   rather than a separate calendar system.
4. **Contact** — phone, WhatsApp, email visible at all times; a builder's leads
   arrive by phone more than by form.

## 9. FAQ

**You were incorporated in 2025 — how do I know you're any good?**
Fair question, and we'd rather you asked it. Delux Construction is new as a company;
the people are not. We set it up specifically to put building, electrical and
plumbing work under one roof instead of subcontracting it out. Ask us for addresses
of finished jobs and we'll give you them.

**Do you really do the electrics and plumbing yourselves?**
Yes. We're registered for electrical installation and for plumbing, heating and
air-conditioning as well as domestic building work. All certification supplied.

**Do you handle planning and building control?**
Yes — drawings, planning or permitted development, structural calculations and
building control sign-off. Most side-returns go through under permitted development.

**How do you price?** Fixed written quote after a site visit and drawings, itemised
by stage. Staged payments against work completed. No deposit before we start on site.

**Are you insured?** £5m public liability, employer's liability, and all work
guaranteed. Certificates on request.
